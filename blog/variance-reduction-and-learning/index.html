Reinforcement learning

Learning more from each sample

A short introduction to variance reduction and why sample efficiency matters in reinforcement learning.

The role of uncertainty

Learning from samples means working with noisy information. A single observation can be useful without being a reliable guide on its own. The question is how to combine observations so that an algorithm makes consistent progress.

Why variance reduction?

Variance reduction aims to make learning updates less noisy. In my work on cascade Q-learning, algorithm design and sample-complexity analysis go together: the goal is to understand how much data is needed to estimate a good decision rule.

From an algorithm to evidence

A theoretical guarantee describes the setting in which a method works. Numerical experiments then help examine its behavior and compare it with alternative algorithms. Both are useful when assessing a new learning method.
